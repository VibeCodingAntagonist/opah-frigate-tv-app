# Opah 0.7.0

Opah 0.7.0 brings messages from Home Assistant to your TV and puts both of your
server connections in one place.

- Pause both notification sources for 15 minutes or 1 hour from
  **Connections** > **Notifications**, without changing recordings or home
  automations. Resume early whenever you want; paused alerts are not replayed.

- Follow guided setup for either service or both, review privacy, and choose
  which notifications to allow. Resume unfinished steps or check your saved
  connections without signing in again. Fictional previews and real Home
  Assistant delivery checks have separate results.

- Open **Connections** from the side menu to set up Frigate or Home Assistant.
  Connection settings use the same PIN protection as Settings.
- Connect Home Assistant and choose this TV in your automations to show text
  notifications while watching cameras or using another TV app.
- Enable optional Home Assistant pictures and up to three automation buttons
  separately. Pictures follow your privacy settings, and buttons require
  Opah's dedicated PIN protection. Home Assistant decides what to do with
  each button response.
- Message details start hidden. Turn them on in **Connections** >
  **Home Assistant** when you want the notification text visible.
- Home Assistant notifications reconnect after a network interruption. Messages
  arrive while the TV is on and connected; there is no offline delivery queue.
- Frigate alerts take priority when messages arrive together.

- Choose optional interactive Frigate alerts to watch a recording, open a live
  camera, or snooze that camera. An additional recording-preview option lets
  you watch inside the pop-up, muted initially. Back returns remote control to
  your current app. Passive alerts remain the default.

- Camera video and audio stop when you leave Opah for the Home screen, freeing
  playback resources for other apps. Video keeps playing in a visible
  picture-in-picture window on supported TVs.
- Camera pictures fit their tiles by default, keeping the edges visible.
- Open a camera with **OK**, or hold **OK** for its options, including
  **Refresh preview**. Camera headings stay visible while scrolling.
- Choose **Find similar activity** to open Search and start a visual search
  from that activity immediately. Refreshing or applying filters keeps the
  same starting activity.
- Each camera's existing **Fit video to screen** or **Stretch video to screen**
  choice applies to both previews and live video.

See the [Home Assistant setup guide](../home-assistant.md) for connection steps
and notification options.
