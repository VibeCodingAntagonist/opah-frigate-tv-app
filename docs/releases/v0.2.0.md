# Opah 0.2.0

> This release has been withdrawn. Its APK is no longer available. Install the
> [newest Opah release](https://github.com/VibeCodingAntagonist/opah-frigate-tv-app/releases)
> instead.

Opah brings live cameras, Frigate Review, Birdseye, and system information to
Android TV and Google TV with controls designed for a normal television remote.

## Highlights

- Watch live cameras using H.264 or H.265 video when supported by the TV.
- Hear camera audio, mute it, or use video-only mode for troubleshooting.
- Keep a live camera visible in picture-in-picture while using another app.
- Browse Frigate alerts and detections, open event details, and play recordings.
- Use a familiar recording timeline to move backward or forward.
- View Frigate storage and performance information.
- Choose a light, dark, system, or custom color theme.
- Save the Frigate connection securely and sign in automatically.
- Recover automatically when a live stream is slow to start or freezes.

## Tested with

- Frigate 0.17.2
- The exact Frigate 0.18.0 beta 3 build `344efb6`
- Android TV and Google TV running Android 7.0 or newer
- The 2024 onn. 4K Pro with Android 14
- H.264 and H.265 live video, Birdseye, Review recordings, and
  picture-in-picture

Other devices and Frigate versions may work but have not completed the same
tests.

## Important limitations

- Opah needs an existing Frigate server and does not record cameras by itself.
- Pausing a live camera freezes the picture but does not allow live rewind.
- Camera audio plays through the TV, but two-way talk is not available.
- H.265 support depends on the television. H.264 is the safest choice when a
  camera does not play.
- Camera features such as H.264+, H.265+, and Smart Codec may need to be turned
  off when they prevent reliable playback.

Opah is an independent community project. It is not made, approved, or
supported by the Frigate team.
