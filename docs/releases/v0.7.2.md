# Opah 0.7.2

Opah 0.7.2 adds an optional HTTPS live connection and clearer help when a camera
cannot play on your TV.

- Hold **OK** on a camera tile and choose **Play using HTTPS** to watch one
  camera through your secure Frigate address. This choice lasts for that
  viewing session; ordinary **OK** keeps using the standard connection.
- When a camera omits setup information needed for live playback, Opah explains
  the problem and offers another stream or HTTPS when available.
- Opah checks the TV's reported video-size limits more carefully. If a stream
  is too large, you can choose another configured stream when available.

HTTPS live video requires an authenticated connection the TV can verify and
Frigate 0.18.2 or a compatible later stable 0.18 release. It is for a single
camera in Opah. **Back** leaves the view, closing an open choice dialog first;
**Home** stops the connection. HTTPS does not lower the camera's resolution or
remove the TV's video-format limits.

Camera permissions, PIN protection, guest mode, and normal certificate checks
continue to apply. Video stays between your TV and configured Frigate server,
without an Opah cloud service or a new recording archive.
